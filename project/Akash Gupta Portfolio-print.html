About

Painting as
a way of thinking

Trained visual artist holding a Master of Visual Arts with strong grounding in painting, composition, and Indian contemporary and modern art.

His work spans bold figurative canvases, conceptual compositions, and immersive large-scale murals — united by a vibrant colour sensibility and engagement with the social world.
