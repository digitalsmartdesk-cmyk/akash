Trained visual artist holding a Master of Visual Arts, with a strong grounding in painting, composition, and Indian contemporary and modern art.

His work spans bold figurative canvases, conceptual compositions, and immersive large-scale murals — all united by a vibrant colour sensibility and a deep engagement with the social world around him.
